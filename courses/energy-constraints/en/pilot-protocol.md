## One complete module and willing participants

Invite 4 to 6 willing graduate students or advanced undergraduates in natural sciences after authorization. Participation and feedback should be voluntary; do not imply it affects course standing. Use the existing entry diagnostic to record concentration/amount, integral and log readiness. Record the optional refresher separately. Participants may use the supplied-output route; no Python ability is required.

Bind the exact accepted English source and model manifest/archive hashes, delivered scientific reading status, default receipt, language, facilitator and access routes before starting. A protocol execution log must identify any changed source; do not mix versions silently. Use only ENERGY-E01..05 and the existing 100-point rubric, with the same 195-minute plan plus optional15. Prework/session/follow-up limits and the two forward arithmetic steps stay unchanged.

Both student and OPEN-answer readings remain accessible. Ask participants to retain a first attempt, record consultations/substantive hints and save the revised attempt. This is pacing and evidence collection, not an answer-access restriction. Facilitator records observations while students do the existing tasks; no extra quiz or computation is appended. A brief usability/misunderstanding comment fits within the existing follow-up feedback time.

## Minimal actual evidence to retain

Use anonymous pilot IDs and permission to retain deidentified work. Record: exact version/language and route; entry diagnostic/refresher use; task/prework/session/follow-up times and interruptions; first/revised component scores and the three conceptual gates; answer/hint consultation timing; blocked file/formula/support steps; and a student's own short feedback statement. Store actual work/feedback only after consent and authorization; keep identity/contact data outside teaching artifacts.

Code misunderstandings from actual attempts: chemical/transformed or dimensional-log mixing; amount/volume mismatch; gross equals net; heat/ATP equals Gibbs availability; telescoping equals accuracy; preceding null power treated as zero; initial-$C_0$ replaced by drifting total; or supplied-output analysis claimed as independent solver/field validation. A code is a facilitator category, not evidence that any student made that mistake. Quote actual permitted feedback with its context; never supply fabricated example responses as observed data.

## Predeclared success criteria

Before contact, adopt or explicitly revise these proposed criteria with the facilitator:

- At least 4 participants provide complete first-attempt, consultation and revision records for all five tasks.
- At least 75% complete the existing module without an unresolved file/worksheet blocker.
- At least 75% reach 70/100 and all three gates after documented revision. Report first-attempt scores separately; this supported result is not unaided mastery.
- Median total time excluding breaks/interruptions is at most 125% of the corresponding planned active workload. The plan includes a 10-minute break, so active base time is 185 minutes; optional refresher use is reported separately and compared to its own added allowance. Report full elapsed time as well, without relabeling it planned time.
- No unresolved critical wording/units/null/support ambiguity prevents interpretation after targeted feedback.

For a small pilot, report denominators, individual deidentified patterns and missing data; do not infer population efficacy, statistical significance or durable retention from these thresholds. Lack of complete records leaves the learner gate unassessed, not passed.
