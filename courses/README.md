# Modeling course

The course turns selected questions from the narrative into small models whose assumptions, units and accounting can be inspected. Read the book for the story; use the modules to make predictions, check calculations and test what a simplified model can support.

## Available modules

1. [Oxygen diffusion and consumption](oxygen-diffusion/README.md): read a steady porewater profile, account for diffusive supply and aggregate consumption, and distinguish an identifiable ratio from separate rate and diffusivity parameters.
2. [Advection, dispersion and parent loss](advection-dispersion/README.md): follow a finite-column pulse, separate physical and numerical spreading, and account for input, export, parent loss and retained storage.

Each module supplies English and Russian readings, exercises, separately titled open worked answers, a verified model and saved outputs. The supplied tables support a route without Python. Attempt the exercises before consulting the answers and record what help you used. Access to answers does not establish unaided mastery.

Both modules' English exercises and answers are included in the book's default HTML/PDF/EPUB editions. Russian materials currently cover the two modules and their new explanatory fragments. They do not constitute a complete Russian edition. Numerical verification concerns the declared equations and scenarios; it does not establish validity in a natural system. Learner protocols remain unexecuted.

## Planned sequence

The following blocks are a development sequence, not available or verified modules:

1. **Saturation kinetics:** distinguish a mechanistic Michaelis-Menten approximation from an empirical Monod response and track substrate depletion under stated capacity assumptions.
2. **Energy constraints:** connect rate expressions to a declared reaction basis, activities and available work, with an explicit domain for forward and reverse reactions.
3. **Growth, yield and maintenance:** couple substrate accounting to biomass, distinguish growth from maintenance and decay, and examine what observations can identify.
4. **Coupled redox processes:** combine a bounded set of donor and acceptor balances and test when supply or inhibition changes the predicted ordering.
5. **Mineral and methane applications:** add selected reaction closures only after their accounting and prerequisite models are established.

The proposed order adds one dependency at a time. It is not a promise that one simple rate law transfers unchanged to every metabolism. Each new block needs a declared model contract, checked sources, numerical verification, exercises and open answers, aligned Russian materials and rendered review before joining the available modules.

Whole-book scientific and editorial review, the remaining Russian chapters and appendices, and actual learner pilots are separate unfinished work. The two accepted modules do not certify every inherited equation or claim in the book.
