## Contract requirements already met

The original model-contract.md, analytical-checks.md and benchmark-plan.md explicitly separate the core rectangular flux-fed pulse from the constant-feed finite steady reference. The D_L=0 characteristic solution is an auxiliary hyperbolic inflow problem, without an independent outflow gradient. The periodic cosine and its semidiscrete reference have different IC/BC and establish smooth operator/time accuracy, not correct assembly of the finite-column boundaries. Finite steady B02/B03 and pulse budget B05 test those boundaries separately.

The core model uses resident porewater C, bulk storage phi*C, u as porewater velocity, v_D=phi*u as Darcy flux, F=phi*(u*C-D_L*C_x), and signed bulk loss -phi*k*C. The inlet prescribes F(0)=v_D*C_in; the exit has C_x(L)=0 and F(L)=v_D*C(L). It is open to advective export. k is aggregate parent disappearance, without products, elemental balance or a unique biological mechanism.

Admissible parameters: finite L>0, 0<phi<=1, u>0, D_L>0, k>=0, C_ref>0, T_p>0; finite h>0 and time step>0 for future discretization. Zero feed is a verification branch. u=0 and D_L=0 are explicitly labeled auxiliary problems. Candidate illustrative scales and optional stress ranges remain proposed in the original contract. Reject nonfinite/invalid inputs and unsupported reverse flow; do not implement negative u by silently changing the boundary roles.

## Feed support and compatible time accounting

Define C_in(t)=C_ref for 0<=t<T_p and zero otherwise. Endpoint values have no effect on the continuum integral. This half-open convention specifies labels and sampling unambiguously; it leaves the original pulse problem unchanged. For a step [t_n,t_{n+1}], use the exact overlap length ell_n=max(0,min(t_{n+1},T_p)-max(t_n,0)). The interval-average feed is C_ref*ell_n/Delta t and input increment is v_D*C_ref*ell_n. A step spanning T_p must retain this amount; aligned steps are convenient but not compulsory.

For the proposed backward-Euler finite-volume scheme, use right-endpoint state/flux for internal faces, outlet and loss. Export both interval-average inlet and right-endpoint outlet, marking the different time supports. Set Delta O_n=Delta t*v_D*C_N^{n+1}, Delta B_n=Delta t*k*M^{n+1}, and retain M^{n+1}. The exact discrete budget is M^{n+1}-M^n=Delta I_n-Delta O_n-Delta B_n. No trapezoidal sink correction, clipping or rescaling is allowed. Budget closure alone is not PDE accuracy.

## Observable definitions for AD-E03 through AD-E05

Resident profiles contain cell averages over declared [x_left,x_right], not disguised point samples. For positive M, a spatial centroid and width may be calculated for the supplied piecewise-constant reconstruction: x_bar=phi*sum(h*C_i*x_i)/M and sigma_x^2=phi*sum(h*C_i*((x_i-x_bar)^2+h^2/12))/M. The h^2/12 term retains each cell's internal geometric variance. Width has metres, not seconds. This describes material still inside the finite column. Export preferentially removes material, so its width is not an unbounded spreading measurement or a direct estimate of D_L. At M=0, centroid/width are undefined, not zero.

The primary learner arrival observable is outlet mass quantiles. With cumulative O(T)>0, define G_T(t)=O(t)/O(T) and t_p by G_T(t_p)=p for p=0.1,0.5,0.9; optionally report t_90-t_10 in seconds. These are conditional on the exported mass and declared observation window T. They are not fractions of the injected mass. Also report O(T)/I_total, B(T)/I_total and M(T)/I_total so conditional timing cannot hide unrecovered mass. If O(T)=0, quantiles are undefined. With D_L>0, a parabolic dispersive model has no sharp positive first-arrival time; do not infer u from a first nonzero floating-point value. Threshold detection would require its own declared threshold and support. Only the D_L=0 auxiliary pulse has the exact sharp delay L/u.

For outlet temporal mean/standard deviation, reconstruct interval export as constant flux on each interval: weights w_n=Delta O_n; first time moment per weight is (t_n+t_{n+1})/2; second moment is (t_n^2+t_n*t_{n+1}+t_{n+1}^2)/3. Divide weighted moments by O(T), then calculate variance. Quantiles use the same piecewise-linear cumulative export. These are reported discrete diagnostics with time support, not exact continuum pulse moments. Positive loss can select shorter-residence exported material, so a narrower reactive breakthrough need not mean a smaller physical dispersion coefficient. Peak effluent concentration must also state whether it is a right-endpoint sample or an interval mean.

## Bounded scenario and exercise dependencies

- AD-E01: conservative core pulse. Predict motion using u; distinguish v_D. Comparing phi at fixed u is different from comparing phi at fixed v_D. A nominal L/u is an advective scale, not an exact dispersive quantile.
- AD-E02: derive the finite slab and per-area budget with the actual flux inlet and open exit. Inlet C_in and resident C(0) must not be conflated. A Dirichlet reservoir is a different, legitimate problem whose actual dispersive input must be counted.
- AD-E03: inspect B02/B03 finite steady boundaries and B05 pulse budget, then separate spatial and semidiscrete temporal refinements. Use the auxiliary zero-dispersion/periodic B06 only for its stated limit or smooth spreading diagnostic. Report nonnegativity without clipping and the fixed-grid continuum-error plateau. No claim of second-order overall advection accuracy.
- AD-E04: propose four cases, all with the same physical pulse duration, concentration, geometry and porosity: k=0 baseline; positive-k baseline; conservative 2*u; conservative 2*D_L. Change one coefficient per comparison. Their numerical values and teaching resolution await acceptance. Do not keep T_p/tau_a fixed when changing u, because that changes the physical injection. Dose scales with v_D when u changes: normalize recovery by each case's own injected amount and report its absolute dose too. Width/arrival use the definitions above; conservation separates storage, export and disappeared parent.
- AD-E05: compare a small scenario set, without an optimizer, to explicitly synthetic interval-averaged outlet concentration or interval exported amount. Known inputs: geometry, phi, measured-or-synthetic declared flow, D_L, feed history and observation support. Let k be the only candidate-changing loss parameter for this bounded inference task. Select the synthetic truth and candidate values only after numerical acceptance. Keep a noiseless reference distinct from any later documented perturbation/noise; no unspecified random errors. Synthetic agreement is a controlled model comparison, not parameter recovery from nature.
- AD-E06: use checked P02, Gouze et al. (2021), for a bounded groundwater-transfer critique of constant D_L. Source appraisal and admissible claim are in primary-boundary-and-e06-readback.md. P03 is optional abstract-only context, not the compulsory methods evidence. No extra exercise, groundwater solver or new empirical fit is introduced.

For E05, the interval observation is y_j=[integral_(a_j)^(b_j) F_out dt]/[v_D*(b_j-a_j)] in mol m^{-3}; an exported-amount observation instead has mol m^{-2}. Evaluate the prediction on those same intervals. Export signed residual prediction minus observation, original units and optionally RMSE of those residuals. A noiseless synthetic task has no invented measurement standard deviation or inferential confidence interval. If noise is later introduced, preserve its exact generator, seed and uncertainty model; divide by sigma only if sigma is actually supplied and justified. Changing support, dilution or normalizing each curve to its peak can conceal mass/arrival disagreement and is not an equivalent observable.

## Accuracy targets and export dependency

Retain the proposed benchmark residual, positivity and smooth-order gates. Independently refine h at sufficiently small time error and Delta t at fixed h; one paired refinement path cannot establish both orders. For future teaching diagnostics propose a difference gate abs(A_refined-A_base)<=0.02*max(abs(A_refined),A_floor), with explicit scales: A_floor=0.01*C_ref for outlet peak, 0.01*tau_a for time quantiles/temporal width, and 0.01*L for spatial width. These are proposed resolution targets, not observed accuracy. Use the unchanged physical reference tau_a=L/u for each comparison. Report changes in recovery/loss/storage fractions with an absolute 0.005 target alongside the stricter discrete budget residual; this separates curve/recovery accuracy from algebraic closure. Revisit the criteria if near-zero signals are pedagogically central. Numerical values, attainable errors, default mesh and cost remain unaccepted until actual verification.

A finite-window recovery/width is valid when labeled as such. To describe effectively complete conservative washout, propose M(T)/I_total<=0.001 and demonstrate quantile/width stability under an extended window. For loss cases, report M(T)/I_total and the open-tail sensitivity as well; complete observation cannot be inferred from a small exported curve. These are future criteria, not proof that the initially proposed 3*tau_a is sufficient.
