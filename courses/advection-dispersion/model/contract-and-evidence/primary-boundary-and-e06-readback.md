(2021), full primary publisher article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/water/articles/10.3389/frwa.2021.766338/full), DOI [10.3389/frwa.2021.766338](https://doi.org/10.3389/frwa.2021.766338). Sections 2.1 to 2.3 describe generated sandstone-like geometries, Stokes flow and time-domain random-walk solute transport. Sections 3.4 and 4 assess breakthrough tails and the approach to asymptotic dispersion. These numerical experiments show microstructure-dependent pre-asymptotic behavior in their studied media. They do not measure our finite column or validate an aquifer prediction. Admissible exercise claim: a constant Fickian D_L is a scale/regime closure that should be tested when transferring a reference column model to heterogeneous groundwater. Proposed independent test: conservative-tracer breakthrough at multiple distances with known injection/flow, recovery and low-concentration uncertainty; compare late-time residuals and fitted transport behavior across distances. Persistent structured tails or changing inferred dispersion would challenge this closure, but would not uniquely identify immobile storage or reaction. This test is proposed, not performed. The source's pore-scale Péclet number differs from module uL/D_L; do not transfer its thresholds or coefficients directly.
