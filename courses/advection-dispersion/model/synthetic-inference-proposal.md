# Bounded synthetic comparison proposal for AD-E05

Status: supplied scientific inputs/conditional comparison, awaiting final numerical/teaching acceptance. No optimizer, field data, empirical calibration, confidence interval or learner result.

Truth is the accepted hypothetical reactive finite-column model: L=1 m, phi=0.30, u=10^{-5} m s^{-1}, D_L=10^{-6} m^2 s^{-1}, k=10^{-5} s^{-1}, C_ref=1 mol m^{-3} porewater, rectangular flux feed T_p=20000 s, zero initial interior and zero-gradient advective outlet. Generate with the same current code at N=800, dt=50 s, window 600000 s. The observations are noiseless interval averages of outlet flux-normalized concentration over eight stated 10000-second intervals. They are not samples from a field or external laboratory. No random generator or seed is used because no noise is added.

Candidates use the proposed teaching N=400, dt=100 s at known identical flow/transport/geometry/feed and the same outlet observation support. Only k changes: 0, 10^{-5}, 2*10^{-5} s^{-1}. Retain absolute source series to reproduce interval averages. Scores are RMSE of prediction minus synthetic observation, in mol m^{-3} porewater, with equal weighting of the eight declared intervals. No uncertainty weighting is introduced. A small nonzero matching-k residual reflects different discretization, not experimental noise or failed empirical fit.

Raw student inputs: observations-synthetic.csv and the separately pinned synthetic-source series. Open completed comparisons: candidate-residuals-open-answers.csv and candidate-scores-open-answers.csv. Generation case/source/input/output hashes are in synthetic-provenance.json and the packet manifest. Known synthetic truth should remain identifiable as provenance, even when an exercise invites the learner to calculate the score before reading its answer.

Time support is fixed and documented; there are no missing observations. A lower peak, incomplete export window, transport broadening or unknown dose would provide alternative explanations if the known conditions were relaxed. Constant phi cancels from C at fixed porewater u. At steady state, simultaneous rescaling of u,D_L,k preserves resident concentrations; this is separately demonstrated in B08. One finite-window scalar or a visually good curve does not identify all those quantities. This proposal conditions on transport and compares only k, without asserting unique practical identifiability outside its small candidate set.

Proposed independent observation for a real future study: conservative-tracer transport with measured flow/injection, followed by a reactive parent with products or balanced chemical observables and an uncertainty/recovery protocol. A held-out synthetic numerical perturbation could test internal robustness, but remains synthetic and cannot validate against nature. No such field protocol was executed and no additional raw-data intake is part of this module.

First-order disappeared parent aggregates possible sinks; the model does not attribute biodegradation, cell survival, complete mineralization, donor/acceptor limitation or water safety. P02's pore-scale numerical results supply a closure critique, not an empirical dataset for these candidate scores.
