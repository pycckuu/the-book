2. C6 July 2019: v1 DOU 10.281, TOU 22.69 mmol $m^{-2} d^{-1}$ and OPD 3.75 mm; v2 DOU 7.26378779, TOU 25.12771396 and OPD 3.0 mm. V2 DOU/TOU = 0.289074756. DOU is inferred from a resolved diffusive oxygen gradient. TOU is chamber uptake and can include other transport and sinks. An interpretation of the difference needs the measurement and correction method.
3. The illustrative model gives 1.37556369 mmol $m^{-2} d^{-1}$. Its inputs were not measured at C6, its surface concentration is imposed, its lower boundary is sealed, and its sink is spatially constant first order. No site-specific transport, porosity, boundary-layer or uncertainty fit was performed. Comparing these unequal conditions is diagnostic, not a validation or fitted parameter.
4. The mean of seven archived v2 station ratios is 0.292795523; the ratio of sums is 0.283052007. The first weights each station equally, the second weights by TOU. The publication reports 0.33 ± 0.07. Do not label that difference rounding error. Section 4.1 discusses a 2.3 mmol $m^{-2} d^{-1}$ chamber-water respiration subtraction based on earlier work because the contemporaneous water-control closure failed. In this packet no additional correction is imposed on the archived workbook. Exact reconciliation of every station with the publication remains unresolved. B5 2019 is excluded only from paired ratios because DOU is missing, not from the original archive. D5 2019 has a measurement caveat.
5. Required for a profile fit: calibrated oxygen-depth observations and uncertainties, surface-interface registration, temperature/salinity, saturation state and porosity, an independently constrained $D_s$, boundary-water and surface concentrations, flow/bioirrigation screening, a stated detection limit, and a reason for the lower boundary. Estimate $\alpha$ first. Hold out independent cores or a separately measured boundary perturbation with time-series profiles and fluxes; propagate parameter and measurement uncertainty, then test predictions without re-fitting. Independent sink attribution additionally needs donor/product or isotope/biological evidence. None of these extra measurements is invented here.

Suggested 10-point rubric: version identity and hashes (2), correct row/units/ratio (2), observable compatibility and illustrative-input limitation (2), aggregation/correction issue without forced reconciliation (2), identifiability and independent validation design (2). A numerically correct answer that treats synthetic data as field measurements cannot earn full credit.
