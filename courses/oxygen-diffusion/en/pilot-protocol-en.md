# Oxygen module: learner-pilot protocol

Revision O2-PILOT-0.1, 10 October 2026. Status: prepared, not run. No students have been contacted. No feedback, learning gains, completion rates or language comparisons are available.

## Question and scope

Can willing graduate and advanced undergraduate natural-science students use one complete oxygen module to move from a porewater profile to a defensible model calculation and a bounded inference? This is a small formative pilot for revising materials, not a controlled efficacy study.

Run only after the editor accepts a pinned English student packet, separate instructor packet, model release, and aligned Russian translation. Any future recruitment or outreach requires separate authority. A facilitator may use this protocol with already willing learners after that authorization. Participation must be voluntary, unrelated to grades, and may stop at any time.

## Participants and language

Target 4 to 6 learners with varied natural-science backgrounds, ideally including both advanced undergraduates and graduate students. Where willing learners are available, include at least two English-packet and two Russian-packet attempts. Do not force students into their weaker language for comparison. Record chosen packet language, broad study level and prerequisite diagnostic only. If just one language is represented, report that coverage gap and do not infer translation equivalence.

Use anonymous IDs P01 onward. Obtain explicit permission to retain anonymized written work and facilitator notes. No audio/video recording is necessary. Keep consent/contacts outside the learning dataset; no names, email addresses, or sensitive demographic information in artifacts. Agree locally on retention and deletion with the participants before collecting attempts. If permission is refused, do not retain that person's material.

## Materials and execution

1. Freeze and hash the student packet, instructor rubric, language mapping, model code, data files and environment instructions. Record the exact bundle with each attempt. Give learners only the student materials.
2. Run the four-item entry diagnostic from the curriculum (10 minutes). Give the documented bridge where needed and record that support; do not exclude those learners from the findings.
3. Ask two baseline questions before instruction: why does oxygen fall with depth, and can a falling profile alone determine respiration? Collect the responses without correction. These are qualitative baselines, not a validated test instrument.
4. Run the 180-minute module with E01 through E07 and the stated break. Learners predict E04 changes before computing. Capture actual time per exercise and assistance. Record student work before corrections.
5. Offer at most one neutral prompt when a learner is stuck: "Which quantity is stored in your slab?" or "What evidence would distinguish those explanations?" Record the exact prompt and when it was given. Scientific correction is allowed afterwards and marked as assistance.
6. Collect remaining E05 calculation and E06 written critique after approximately 90 minutes of follow-up. Record actual timing and missing tasks rather than assigning imagined responses.
7. Score first independent attempts with the frozen rubric. Have a second qualified reader score E02, E05 and E06 without the first score, if available. Reconcile differences using exact answer excerpts. If no second scorer is available, report single-reader scoring.
8. Collect four short feedback responses: hardest step and why; confusing word/equation; what observation would change confidence in the model; one change that would help the next learner. Ask for a concrete place or attempted answer, not only satisfaction.
9. Optional 10-minute delayed transfer 3 to 7 days later: use an unseen surface-oxygen perturbation with the same accepted model. Record whether it happened. Do not treat non-response as either learning or forgetting.

## Success criteria set before attempts

These are provisional design criteria, not outcomes. Report denominators, individual variation, missingness, and assisted vs independent work.

- At least 4 completed attempts are needed to interpret the module pilot; fewer attempts remain useful case observations but cannot satisfy the pilot criterion.
- At least 75% of completed first attempts reach 70/100 and pass the two scientific gates: consistent flux sign and volume basis; no claim that synthetic/schematic agreement validates a real sediment. For n = 4, this means at least 3 learners.
- At least 75% reproduce the documented model and explain the check tolerances in E03 without a facilitator supplying the result. Technical help is logged separately.
- At least 75% identify non-identifiability or an underdetermined inference in E05 and propose a relevant independent measurement.
- Every learner can access the student packet and model output without receiving the instructor solution file. Broken links, unit-formatting errors and solution leakage must be corrected before another run.
- Translation-specific misunderstandings must be documented with an exact term or equation and reviewed against the source text. Absence of reported problems in a small sample is not proof of equivalence.
- Report median and range of observed times. The planned 180 + 90 minutes is a budget to test, not a claimed completion time. If at least half exceed 120% of that budget because of the materials, shorten or scaffold the corresponding step.

## Evidence records

Create one record per actual attempt: anonymous learner_id; attempt date; packet language and hashes; study level; diagnostic responses and bridge support; exercise_id; original answer file; points and gate result; completion minutes; assistance and exact prompt; feedback excerpt (with permission); scorer; revision decision. Use blank fields for missing evidence, never manufactured examples in the results table.

Aggregate evidence: number invited only if actually known; number willing; number started; number completed; score median/range; counts passing each gate; recurring misunderstandings supported by actual attempts. Give counts as well as percentages. This small convenience sample cannot establish causal learning gains, general effectiveness or a Russian-vs-English effect.

## Revision rules

For each revision, cite the attempt and exercise, preserve the original response, state the misunderstanding, propose one teaching change, and check that the fix preserves the verified model's model. Two learners making the same volume-basis error triggers a worked slab scaffold before E02. Two confusing a finite-domain tail with an anoxic boundary triggers an explicit threshold/boundary contrast before E04. Two interpreting synthetic recovery as field validation triggers a provenance classification before E05. A translation discrepancy in any numerical condition triggers alignment QA before further use.

Change instructions only after scoring the frozen attempts. Version the revised packet and change log; never silently amend the source learners saw. Re-test changed exercises with willing learners under separately authorized access and record actual responses. the editor receives the evidence-backed revision proposal and coverage limits.
